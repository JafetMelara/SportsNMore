Basketball

Basketball is a fast-paced sport that has been around for a long time, its intensity captures many people leading it to become one of the most popular sports in the world. The game is usually played 5 vs 5 in offical matches however it can also be played in smaller matches even up to 1 vs 1. The goal or the way to win the game in this sport is to get the ball in the net. There are different ways to do this and you are awarded different number of points based on your shot.

Michael Jordan

Widely regarded as the greatest of all time, winning 6 championships and multiple MVPs with his legendary intensity.

LeBron James

Known for his incredible longevity and leadership, carrying teams to the finals and becoming the all-time leading scorer.

Legends of the Game

Two of the greatest players to ever grace this sport are Michael Jordan and Lebron James. Michael Jordan was one of the first players to be recognized as the best of all time, his intensity towards his opponents and even his teammates was idolized and people loved this. He went on to win 6 championships multiple MVPS. Lebron James was the next person to be recognized as good or better then Michael Jordan, Lebron is recognized by his longetivty and how he can carry his teammates to the finals with a lack of help. He is the leading scorer in all history and has 4 championships.

The Debate

The debate of who the best player in the history of basketball is between Michael Jordan and Lebron James is one of the most heated debates in sports history. Both players have their own accomomplishments and accolades, and since they have never played against each other in their primes it is hard to say who is better.
