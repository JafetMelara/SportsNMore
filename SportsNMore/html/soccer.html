History of Soccer

Exploring the greatest players, iconic moments, and the biggest tournament in soccer.

Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi is considered one of the greatest soccer players of all time. He is known for his incredible dribbling, passing and his ability to score goals. Messi has won many titles with his main team Barcelona He also has had personal success winning the Ballon d'Or multiple times, which is awarded to the best player in the world, as well as golden boot.

Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo is another soccer player who is considered to be the greatest of all time. He is known for his incredible athleticism, his work ethic, and his power. He has had numerous teams but his main team has been Real Madrid. Ronaldo just like Messi has won the Ballon d'Or multiple times and has also won the golden boot. Ronaldo and Messi have had a rivalry for over a decade.

The World Cup

The FIFA world cup is the biggest soccer tournament in the woirld, it takes place every 4 years and only national teams can participate. The world cup is watched by millions of poeple around the world and it is hosted by a different country every 4 years. The world cup is every footballers biggest dream.

The Greatest Rivalry

Messi and Ronaldo had one of the greatest rivalries in sports history. For over a decade, both players dominated soccer and pushed each other to become even better. Their rivalry helped make modern soccer even more popular.

Random Fact

Football is called soccer in North America because there was already a sport called football there, while in Europe it is known as football.

Iconic Goal

Maradona's Hand of God is one of the most famous and controversial moments in soccer history.

World Cup Winners
